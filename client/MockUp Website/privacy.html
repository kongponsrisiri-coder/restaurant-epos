Your Data

Privacy & Cookies

Plain-English information about the data we handle when you book, order or get in touch.

Who we are

Baan Siam, 18 Marylebone High Street, London W1U 4LJ. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website. Questions? Email hello@baansiam.co.uk.

What we collect, and why

Table bookings — your name, contact details and booking details, used to manage your reservation. Processed on SiamEPOS, our restaurant management platform.

Online orders — your name, contact details, order and pickup time, used to prepare and hand over your order. Card payments are handled by Stripe; your card details never touch our servers (Stripe privacy policy).

Emails — booking and order confirmations are sent via Brevo, our email provider. Marketing emails are sent only if you ticked the consent box when booking, and every one contains a working unsubscribe link.

We never sell your data.

Cookies

This website sets no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. The booking and ordering widgets use essential browser storage only — for example, to remember your basket while you order. Our typefaces load from Google Fonts; no cookies, though your browser contacts Google's servers.

You can clear or block browser storage in your browser settings at any time.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can request a copy of your personal data, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw marketing consent at any time (the unsubscribe link does this instantly). You can also complain to the ICO.
