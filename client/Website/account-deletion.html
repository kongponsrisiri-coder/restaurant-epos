Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: 30 June 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your SiamEPOS account and the personal data associated with it. SiamEPOS is operated by SiamEPOS Ltd (Company No. 17215576), England & Wales.

How to request deletion

  1. Email info@siamepos.co.uk from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Delete my account".
  2. We will verify that the request comes from the account holder.
  3. We will delete your account and associated personal data, and confirm by email, normally within 30 days.

If you operate a restaurant on SiamEPOS, you can also delete individual customer, booking and order records directly in the app at any time (Admin → Customers / Bills).

What is deleted

  • Your account and login details
  • Staff records (names, roles, PINs, clock records)
  • Customer records you hold in SiamEPOS (names, phone numbers, email addresses, bookings and order history)
  • Menu, settings and other operational data

What is kept, and for how long

Records that we are required by law to retain — in particular transaction and sales records needed for UK tax and accounting purposes — are kept for 7 years in line with HMRC requirements, after which they are deleted. Routine backups are purged on their normal rolling cycle. No data is kept longer than necessary.

Deleting some data without closing your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove specific records — individual customer, booking and order records can be deleted in the app, or you can email us to request removal of specific data.

Need help?

For any account or data deletion request, contact info@siamepos.co.uk
