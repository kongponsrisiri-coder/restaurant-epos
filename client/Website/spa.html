Always up to date
Live appointment timeline — every therapist, every booking

One screen shows every therapist, every booking, every gap — updated in real time. From the office, from home, from anywhere — see exactly what's happening in your spa. Tap any appointment to view client details, start the treatment, or take payment.

Customers choose their therapist online

Your booking widget lets customers pick their treatment, then choose their preferred therapist by name. No more "Can I have Malee?" messages on LINE. No more calling the shop to ask if a specific person is available. They book it themselves, 24 hours a day.

📣
Bring customers back
Client CRM + email campaigns

Every client, every visit, every treatment — all on record. See who hasn't been back in 60 days and send them a win-back offer automatically. Run birthday campaigns. Reward loyal clients. Your customer list belongs to you — not to Treatwell, not to anyone else.

🏥
Required by law
Client medical records — digital, signed

Every client completes a health questionnaire and signs digitally before their first treatment. Pregnancy, heart conditions, skin conditions — all recorded and stored. No more paper forms that get lost. No more liability if something goes wrong and there's no record.

📅
Know who's working
Rota — who works which days, what hours

Set each therapist's weekly schedule once. The online booking widget only shows available slots for therapists who are actually working that day. Change a day off? Override it once. The whole system updates automatically — no manual calendar changes needed.

📊
See your numbers
Daily revenue, top treatments, payment breakdown

Open the app and see today's revenue, how many appointments ran, your top 5 treatments, and how people paid — cash, card, voucher, Treatwell. Which therapist is busiest? Which treatment makes you the most money? Now you know, without doing any maths.
