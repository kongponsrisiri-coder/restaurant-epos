1. The Service

SiamEPOS is a cloud-based restaurant and hospitality management platform operated by Korakot Kongponsrisiri, trading as SiamEPOS ("SiamEPOS", "we", "us", "our"). The service includes, depending on your plan:

  • Point-of-sale (EPOS) and table management
  • Kitchen Display System (KDS) and order routing
  • Online reservation widget and guest management
  • Zero-commission online takeaway ordering widget
  • Inventory management, AI invoice scanning, and cost-vs-sales analytics
  • Customer CRM and email marketing via Brevo
  • Allergen management tools (UK 14-allergen / Natasha's Law)
  • Staff management, clock-in/out, and payroll summaries
  • Offline-capable desktop application (SiamEPOS Pro for Mac and Windows)
  • Bilingual Thai and English interface
  • SiamSpa appointment management (where subscribed)

Feature availability depends on your subscription plan. We reserve the right to add, modify, or discontinue features with reasonable notice.
